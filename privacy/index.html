Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Near Player app (com.nearplayer.app)

What we collect

Account: your email address or Google account email, used only to sign you in.

Profile: name, username, photo, bio, games, platforms, gamer ID and city that you choose to add. Your date of birth is used only to confirm you are 18+ and is never shown to anyone.

Mobile number: verified once by SMS to keep fake accounts out. It is never shown to other players or used for marketing.

Verification selfie: if you want to host or join sessions, you take a live selfie that our safety team compares with your profile photo. It is deleted as soon as it has been reviewed; we keep only the result (verified or not).

Approximate location: when you allow it, we store your location rounded to roughly 500 m. Other players only ever see an approximate distance (for example "3 km"), never your location or address. We do not collect location in the background.

Activity: requests, sessions, chat messages, ratings, blocks and reports you create, and a push-notification token for your device.

How we use it

To show you players and sessions nearby, deliver requests and messages, send notifications you asked for, and keep the community safe (for example, reviewing reports and suspending abusive accounts).

We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

Who can see what

Other signed-in players can see your public profile (name, username, photo, bio, games, platforms, rating, verified badge) and an approximate distance. On the map you appear somewhere within a 1 km area, never at your real location. Your gamer ID is visible on your profile; leave it empty if you prefer.

Chat messages are visible only to the people in that chat. Blocked players cannot see you at all.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, sign-in and storage), Brevo (sign-in emails), Google Firebase (phone verification SMS and push notifications), Expo (push notifications), and Google (optional Google sign-in and maps). They process data only to run the app for us.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, hide yourself from Nearby, turn off location or notifications in your phone settings, and block or report anyone.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. This permanently removes your profile, photo, location, requests, sessions and messages you sent.

For any privacy request or grievance (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), email gupta.mayank201@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account, except reports and safety records we must keep to protect other users or comply with law.

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by row-level security so each user can only read what they are allowed to.

Age

Near Player is only for people aged 18 and over. We remove accounts that appear to belong to minors.

Changes

We may update this policy and will tell you in the app about important changes. Contact: gupta.mayank201@gmail.com.
