Delete your Near Player account

Near Player (com.nearplayer.app) · developer contact: gupta.mayank201@gmail.com

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Near Player and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email (if you can't open the app)

Email gupta.mayank201@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Near Player account", sent from the email address you use to sign in (or your Google account email). We confirm the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, games, platforms, gamer ID, city, date of birth), your approximate location, your push-notification tokens, the requests and sessions you created, the messages you sent and the ratings you gave.

What is kept: safety reports stay in anonymised form (no longer linked to your account) so moderators can keep the community safe, or when the law requires it. Nothing else is kept after deletion.
