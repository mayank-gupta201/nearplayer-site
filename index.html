Built for safe meetups

You are meeting real people, so safety is part of the product, not an afterthought.

  • 18+ only, one account per phone number.
  • Live face check before anyone can host or join an in-person session.
  • Approximate distance only: nobody sees your exact location.
  • Block and report anyone; repeated reports suspend an account.
  • Scam warnings in chat if someone asks for money, OTPs or UPI.
  • Safe-meetup tips and a one-tap 112 emergency shortcut.

FAQ

Is Near Player free?

Yes. No ads, no paid features.

Can people see where I live?

No. Others only see an approximate distance, and map pins are placed somewhere within a 1 km area. Share your address only in chat, only if you want to.

Why do I need a face check?

To host or join in-person sessions, every player verifies their phone and takes two quick live photos. They are compared with the profile photo automatically and deleted right after. It keeps fake profiles out. Browsing and chatting don't need it.

Do I need a console?

No. Many players join someone else's setup or a café session.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. iPhone users can use the web app at app.nearplayer.live.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or see how to delete your account.
